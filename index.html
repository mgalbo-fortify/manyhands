Questions

You decide where AI belongs.

Straight answers to what owners ask us first.

Will customers still deal with a real person?

Yes. For your first project, customer conversations stay with your team. AI helps prepare and organize information behind the scenes.

Will this make our service feel less personal?

We start by finding the parts of your service customers value most, then choose what to automate around them. Your team keeps control of customer-facing messages and decisions.

What if we don't know where to start?

Pick one task your team repeats every week. We will tell you honestly whether AI, a feature in software you already own, or a simpler process is the right fix.

Do we need to buy new software?

Usually not. We start with what you already use, like your email, calendar, CRM or accounting system. If something new would help, we explain the cost before you decide. Any subscription is billed by the vendor, not marked up by us.

What does it cost?

Your first month is $3,500, fixed and agreed in writing before any work begins. Ongoing help is optional at $750 a month. The first conversation is always free.
